Act 03 · AI

No time to think? Let the AI draft it.

Slide from Steady to Moonshot and press Generate. A language model builds a five-coin portfolio for your risk level, with a one-line reason for every pick.

  • Never trusted blindly. The server checks five distinct coins and the FP budget before you see the result.
  • You stay in charge. Use the portfolio as is, or tweak it in the draft.

Act 04 · Get paid

Scored by the program. Paid by the program.

Each coin's result is its % change from the start price to the end price (never worse than −100%). Your score is the sum of your five coins. The Solana program computes it from recorded prices and pays winners straight to their wallets.

  • Ties split the prize. Tied players share their combined slots.
  • Transparent fees. 5% to the platform, plus 5% to the creator in player-made tournaments.
  • Your keys, your wallet. Everything is signed with Mobile Wallet Adapter. The app never holds your keys.
