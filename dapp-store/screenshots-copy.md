# DraftGem — dApp Store screenshot texts (draft, 2026-11-02)

Format as for SwapKings: a big all-caps headline (3-4 lines max) + a short sub-line (2-3 lines).
Sub-lines are kept under ~120 characters. Order tells one story: find -> draft -> AI -> live -> paid -> host -> trust.
Core set = slides 1-7 and 10 (8 slides). 8 and 9 are optional, drop them first if the portal limits the count.

Wording rules (legal): "skill game", "tournament", "prize", "get paid on-chain". Never "bet", "gamble", "jackpot", "win big", "easy money".

---

## 1. Hero — screen: Lobby with a few tournaments
**PICK 5 COINS. BEAT THE FIELD.**
Daily fantasy for crypto, built for Seeker. Draft five Solana coins and compete in tournaments.

## 2. Find a tournament — screen: Lobby with filters visible (All / Top 1 / Top 3 / 30% / 50% / PvP + the selects)
**FIND YOUR TOURNAMENT.**
A new one opens all the time. Filter by prize, fee and time, and join in SKR, SOL, ORE or USDC.

## 3. Draft — screen: Draft page with 3-5 coins picked, budget bar partly filled
**4,000 FP. FIVE COINS. YOUR CALL.**
Calm coins are cheap, wild ones cost more. The budget makes you choose, so knowledge beats recklessness.

## 4. AI portfolio — screen: AI page with the risk slider and a generated portfolio with reasons
**LET AI BUILD YOUR PORTFOLIO.**
Slide from Steady to Moonshot and get five coins with a reason for every pick.

## 5. Live — screen: standings of a running tournament with your portfolio opened
**WATCH IT LIVE.**
Follow the standings as prices move. Your score is the sum of your five coins' moves.

## 6. Prizes — screen: a finished tournament, Prizes tab or "Paid out" result
**GET PAID ON-CHAIN.**
Prizes go from the program's vault straight to your wallet. Pick Top 1, Top 3, 30%, 50% or a PvP duel.

## 7. Host (the % slide) — screen: Create Tournament page with fee, prize structure and the green "Earn 5% of every prize pool" banner visible
**HOST A TOURNAMENT. EARN 5%.**
Choose the entry fee, prizes and schedule. Share the link and keep 5% of every pool you host.

## 8. (optional) Private — screen: the "tournament created" page with the share link, or Create with Private selected
**PLAY WITH FRIENDS.**
Make it private and send the link. Only people who have it can find your tournament.

## 9. (optional) Share — screen: Profile with the profit/loss chart, or the share dialog
**SHARE YOUR RESULTS.**
Track your profit and loss over time and share it as an image.

## 10. Trust — screen: Draft or Results with the SKR balance in the header
**BUILT FOR SEEKER. VERIFIED ON-CHAIN.**
Sign with your wallet through Mobile Wallet Adapter. Entry fees sit in a program vault, and every tournament can be checked on Solscan.

## What to prepare on the Seeker for real screens
- Slide 5 (live) needs a tournament that is running right now with your own entry: enter a 1-hour tournament and screenshot during the round.
- Slide 6 (paid out) needs a finished tournament you played; otherwise use the Results list with a "Paid out" status.
- Slide 7: open "+" and leave the defaults or fill a nice example (name, 100 SKR, 50%).
- Slide 4: generate a Balanced or Bold portfolio so the reasons are visible.
- Hide anything personal (wallet address in the header is fine: it is public, but check the SKR balance you show).
- Screens must be at least 1080 px wide; the Seeker's native screenshots are fine.
